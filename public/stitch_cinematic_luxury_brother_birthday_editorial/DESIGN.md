## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an intimate, cinematic editorial aesthetic celebrating fraternal bonds, shared history, and personal reverence. Drawing inspiration from high-end monographs, private museum catalogues, and prestige film title sequences, the visual narrative avoids standard celebratory cliches. Instead, it frames life milestones with quiet gravitas, warmth, and enduring honor.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with subtle **Cinematic Atmosphere**:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Layered obsidian surfaces paired with extremely subtle film grain textures (opacity 0.02–0.04) evoke an archival, celluloid sensibility.
- **Curated Asymmetry:** Grid compositions alternate between broad negative space and tightly focused vignettes to create rhythmic pacing akin to turning pages of an architectural art book.
- **Restrained Ornamentation:** Hairline metallic accents and precise typographic chapter tags (`VOL. 01`, `CH. IV`, `ARCHIVE // 1994`) balance sentimental warmth with architectural rigor.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in deep, light-absorbing obsidian foundations punctuated by soft bone-cream typography and muted champagne gold touches. Color is deployed conservatively to maintain an aura of permanence and luxury.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#D4AF37`) & Tertiary (`#C5A059`):** Champagne gold used strictly for hairline rules, subtle interactive highlights, focus states, and chapter pagination indices. Never used for large filled blocks or heavy buttons.
- **Secondary (`#F8F6F0`):** Warm ivory applied to editorial display headlines, pull quotes, and hero dates to provide high-contrast legibility without the stark harshness of pure white.
- **Neutral Foundation (`#0B0B0C`, `#070708`):** Deep obsidian serves as the primary canvas, creating an immersive void that recedes into the screen.
- **Structural Charcoals (`#161618`, `#222225`):** Reserved for card containers, secondary surface tiers, and media frames, ensuring visual separation without jarring contrast.
- **Muted Editorial Type (`#8E8D8A`):** Low-contrast silver-ash for long body passages, metadata timestamps, and photo captions.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an intentional tension between poetic romanticism and structured cataloging:

- **Primary Display (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for grand statements, chapter titles, milestones, and personal reflections. Italics are employed sparingly for pull quotes and intimate handwritten-style phrases to soften the tone.
- **Systematic Structure (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Balanced, geometric, and clean. Used with generous letter-spacing (`0.15em` to `0.25em`) in uppercase for navigation, archive numbers, timecodes, and metadata. This introduces a cinematic title sequence quality.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Never pair two serif weights within the same block. Always transition from high-contrast serif headlines into widely spaced, quiet sans-serif body or label tokens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column asymmetric grid that emphasizes editorial breathing room and intentional white (negative) space.

### Structural Rhythm
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, dynamic asymmetric offsets (e.g., imagery spanning 7 columns, accompanying text pushed to an offset 3 columns with a 2-column blank buffer). Standard section padding defaults to `space-3xl` (160px) to give each milestone or memory chapter the presence of a museum installation.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. Asymmetric side-by-side treatments compress to stacked layouts with staggered alignments. Margins scale to `2rem`.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column layout. Content flows sequentially like an unspooling film roll. Margins lock at `1.25rem`, with vertical section gaps maintained at `space-xl` (64px) to preserve drama without overwhelming small displays.
- **Layout Modifiers:** Fine vertical hairline guides (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`) may run between select columns to ground narrative blocks against photographic memories.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic dropping shadows, heavy blur diffusion, or floating Material-style depth tiers. Visual separation and layering are strictly planar and atmospheric:

- **Surface Layering:**
  - `Base`: `#070708` (deep ground).
  - `Canvas Tier`: `#0B0B0C` (primary reading space).
  - `Card / Media Mat`: `#161618` (subtle contrast framing).
  - `Active / Elevated Overlay`: `#222225` with 40% opacity and a subtle `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Hairline Framing:** Depth is defined by precision line work rather than shadows. Containers, modal panels, and lightbox wrappers utilize crisp `1px` borders colored with `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)` or `rgba(248, 246, 240, 0.08)`.
- **Atmospheric Grain:** A persistent CSS SVG film grain overlay applied via `mix-blend-mode: overlay` at 3% opacity ties foreground typography and photographic assets together into a singular cinematic grain structure.
- **Lightbox Scrim:** Fixed background overlay of `#070708` rendered at 92% opacity, obscuring background content to isolate selected memories or video vignettes.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a strict, architectural `0` (Sharp) corner radius across all visual assets:
- **Zero Radius:** Every button, photo mat, lightbox container, card, and rule maintains sharp 90-degree corners (`0px`). This honors traditional publication layouts, photographic darkroom prints, and museum framing aesthetics.
- **Inner Framing:** Archival photos and letter excerpts use inset borders (`inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`) to replicate physical framing mounts.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Strictly prohibited. Badges, tags, and interactive indicators must be expressed as boxed, tracked-out rectangles or raw underlined text.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary CTA:** Architectural sharp-edged outline (`1px solid #D4AF37`) filled with `#0B0B0C`. Text in `label-caps` (`#D4AF37`). On hover: fills to `#D4AF37` with smooth ease (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), text shifts to `#070708`.
- **Secondary Ghost / Text Link:** Unbordered. Upper-case tracked sans-serif with a persistent 1px hairline rule 4px beneath the baseline (`rgba(248, 246, 240, 0.4)`). On hover, underline animates across from left to right in solid `#D4AF37`.

### Cards & Narrative Panes
- **Memory Card:** Background `#161618`, border `1px solid rgba(248, 246, 240, 0.06)`. Internal padding `space-lg`. Media is inset without rounding. Includes metadata header (`ARCHIVE ENTRY // NO. 04`) in `label-caps` colored in `#C5A059`.
- **Quote Card:** Full-bleed dark charcoal block with left hairline border (`2px solid #D4AF37`). Displays serif quotes in `#F8F6F0` alongside an author attribution in muted sans-serif.

### Chips & Chapter Indicators
- **Chapter Marker:** Borderless, minimalist rectangular pill with a preceding golden em-dash or hairline dot (`— CHAPTER III`). Font size `11px`, letter-spacing `0.25em`.
- **Filter Tags:** Sharp bordered boxes (`1px solid rgba(248, 246, 240, 0.15)`), background transparent, hover fill `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.05)`.

### Media & Lightbox
- **Interactive Photo Tile:** Aspect ratios locked strictly to `4:5`, `1:1`, or `16:9`. Subtle desaturation filter (20%) that transitions to full color on cursor focus. Clicking triggers the lightbox.
- **Lightbox Modal:** Fullscreen obsidian overlay. Media presented within a thin champagne border (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`), flanked by an editorial narrative panel detailing the date, location, and heartfelt brotherly note in `body-lg`.

### Form & Wish Inputs (Private Guestbook / Birthday Note)
- **Inputs:** Minimalist underline style. Flat transparent background, bottom border `1px solid rgba(248, 246, 240, 0.2)`. Focus transitions border to `#D4AF37`. Typography uses `body-md` in `#F8F6F0`. No rounded inputs or floating bubbles.
- **Radio & Checkboxes:** Custom square selectors (`14px x 14px`) with sharp corners and `1px` borders. Checked state features a centered `6px x 6px` gold square fill.
